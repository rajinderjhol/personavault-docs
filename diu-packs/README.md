# Domain Intelligence Unit (DIU) packs

A DIU pack is a vertical declaration of vocabulary, decision kinds, evidence sources, and AI action perimeter. It does not add new substrate code; it tells the same governed-decision harness what a decision *means* in a domain.

This folder publishes one evidence-led paper per released pack. Each paper states:

1. what domain decision the pack governs;
2. what the pack declares;
3. what is proven by compiler, conformance vectors, or receipts;
4. what the pack explicitly does **not** claim;
5. what a builder or reviewer can reuse.

Papers here are not marketing briefs and not certifications. `STATUS.md` wins on conflicts.
